The Experience

Little rituals, grand details

From the moment the doors open, everything is arranged around your comfort.

Skyline Terrace Pool

An infinity-edge pool on the 21st floor with panoramic views of the city lights.

Golden Spa & Hammam

Signature gold-infused therapies, sauna and a traditional hammam for two.

Leaf & Flame Restaurant

Farm-to-table dining with a wood-fired kitchen, led by executive chef Elif Karam.

Sunrise Yoga Studio

Daily guided yoga at dawn on the terrace, followed by a cold-pressed juice bar.

Private Chauffeur

Airport transfers and city tours in our classic fleet, on call around the clock.

Atelier Breakfast

A daily spread of local pastries, fresh seafood and made-to-order eggs until noon.
